# FontOdyssey Small-and-Beautiful Curation Policy V1

FontOdyssey is a curated popular-font download site, not a comprehensive font
mirror. The working target is 250 legally preflighted font families, with a
150-family launch set and a 100-family reserve set.

Selection uses a separate curation score:

- Official Google Fonts popularity: 60%
- Strategic language value: 20%
- Page-depth potential: 15%
- License confidence: 5%

Portfolio quotas prevent popularity alone from flooding the catalog with one
category. The 250-family library targets 95 Sans Serif, 50 Serif, 45 Display,
35 Handwriting, and 25 Monospace families, including 50 CJK families. All
available Chinese families that pass license preflight are treated as core.

The curation score does not replace the SEO Opportunity Score. Search Demand
and SERP Opportunity remain `UNASSESSED` until supported by evidence. Selection
means `SELECTED_FOR_SCORING`, not downloaded or Approved.
